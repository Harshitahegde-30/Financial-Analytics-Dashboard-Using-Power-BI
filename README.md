The project entails developing an interactive financial sales dashboard using Power BI for business analysis in terms of products, countries and customers.

Objectives:
The main objectives of this project are as follows:
To monitor the financial performance of the company as a whole
To analyze sales and profit by product and country
To understand sales trends over a period of time
To evaluate the impact of discount on revenue as well as profit
To identify the high-performing segments of customers

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard shows key financial metrics such as:
Sales amd Sales LY
Profit and Profit LY
Profit Margin and Profit Margin LY
Units Sold

Sales Analysis
The visualizations used for this include:
Sales by Country
Monthly Sales Trend
Sales by Customer Segment
Orders by Country
Profit margin by Country
% of Discount offered by Discount Band
Profit margin by Segment

From the analysis:
There are variations in sales performance, both across different countries and customers.
Discounts affect sales as well as profitability.

Tools & Technologies Used
Microsoft Power BI
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

Business Intelligence Techniques
